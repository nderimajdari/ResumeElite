The Executive resume template

For directors, vice presidents and C-suite candidates. A centred serif nameplate sets a formal tone; the main column carries your leadership experience, and a narrow side column holds expertise, languages and board or certification credentials.

  • Best forDirector, VP, C-suite, general manager, partner
  • StructureSerif nameplate, wide main column, narrow side column
  • LengthTwo pages is normal at this level

What an executive resume has to prove

At senior level the question is no longer "can you do the job" but "what will be different after you arrive". Every entry should show scope and outcome.

Scope, on the first line under each title:

VP Operations, Northwind Logistics                     2019 - Present
Led 1,200 staff across 9 sites; P&L $340M; reported to the CEO.

Then three or four outcomes, with the numbers a board would care about:

Turned a $12M operating loss into a $9M profit in two years.

Led the integration of two acquisitions; retained 94% of key staff.

The executive summary

Three or four lines at the top that position you: your function, the size of organisations you have led, and the kind of problem you are known for solving (turnarounds, scale-ups, international expansion). Avoid adjectives such as "visionary" or "results-driven" and let the numbers say it.

Two pages is fine

Recruiters expect two pages from senior candidates. Keep the last fifteen years in detail and summarise earlier roles in a short "Earlier career" line.

Board roles and education

Board seats, advisory roles and executive education (an MBA or an executive programme) go in the side column, where they are visible without taking space from your results.
