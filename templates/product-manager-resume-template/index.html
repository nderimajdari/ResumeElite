The Product Manager resume template

Product hiring managers read for outcomes and judgement. This template opens with Product Impact cards — two to four numbers you moved — and then a clean timeline of roles with a small role pill under your name.

Impact cards

Use the Achievements section. Put the result in the title and the context in the description:

+31% activation          rebuilt onboarding around the first key action
$2.1M new ARR            usage-based pricing, launched Q2
-40% support tickets     self-serve billing flows

Choose numbers you can explain in depth, because the interview will go there.

Writing PM experience

One line of scope (product area, team size, users), then bullets in the shape problem, decision, result. Show the decision — what you chose not to build is often more convincing than what you shipped.

Cut the onboarding flow from 9 steps to 4 after interviews showed setup, not pricing, was the blocker. Activation +31% in a quarter.

Launches

The Projects section is titled Launches. Use it for zero-to-one products or big releases, with the date and adoption figure.

Moving into product

From engineering, design or analytics: frame past work in product terms — users, trade-offs, metrics — and put any product-facing project first.

Frequently asked questions

Outcomes you influenced, the decisions behind them, and the scope you owned. Feature lists without results read as project management rather than product management.

Two to four headline numbers in the impact cards and roughly one per bullet in experience. Every number should be one you can explain in an interview.
