The Freelancer resume template

For independent contractors, consultants and freelancers. An Available for projects tag sits above your name, Selected Work comes before job history, and a warm side rail lists your services, contact details and languages, so a client sees what you offer before anything else.

A freelance resume sells a service

An employer asks "can this person do the job?". A client asks "can this person solve my problem, and have they done it before?". The layout is built for the second question: work first, then the services you offer, then your history.

Selected Work

Three to five projects, each with the client (or "a fintech start-up" if under NDA), what you delivered and the result:

Redesigned onboarding for a meal-kit app; trial-to-paid conversion rose from 9% to 14%.

Wrote and produced 12 product videos for a B2B SaaS launch; 220k views in the first month.

Services

List what clients can hire you for, in their language: "Brand identity", "Shopify store builds", "Technical SEO audits". Avoid internal jargon.

How to list freelance work as experience

Treat your freelance practice as one job:

Freelance Product Designer                         2021 - Present
Clients include a fintech start-up, a national retailer and two agencies.

That avoids a page of short contracts, and recruiters read it as continuous employment, which it is.
