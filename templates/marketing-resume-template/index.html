Campaigns before job titles

Marketing managers hire on work. Use the Projects section — it is titled Campaigns here — for two or three pieces with a result:

Spring relaunch — Paid social, CRM. Integrated campaign that lifted trial sign-ups 41% in six weeks on a flat budget.

Name the channel and the budget scale if you can. A link to the live work or a case study belongs in the project URL.

Metrics that marketers trust

CAC, ROAS, conversion rate, pipeline sourced, organic traffic, email revenue share. Pick the ones your role actually owned. "Increased brand awareness" is not a metric.

Skills by channel

Group them: paid (Meta, Google, TikTok), lifecycle (Klaviyo, Braze), analytics (GA4, Looker), content and SEO (Ahrefs, Webflow). The bars give a quick read of where your depth is — be honest, because you will be asked.

Colour

The default rose suits consumer and creative brands. For B2B or fintech, switch the accent to ink or blue; the layout stays the same.
