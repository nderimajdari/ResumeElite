What hospitality managers look for

Presentation, reliability and guest outcomes. The design handles presentation; your content needs to cover the other two.

Guest outcomes you can quote

Review scores, mystery-shopper results, upsell revenue, repeat-guest rate, covers per night, events delivered:

Raised the property's review score from 8.6 to 9.3 by redesigning the arrival experience and training 22 front-desk staff.

Systems

Opera, Mews, Cloudbeds, Micros, Toast, SevenRooms — property and point-of-sale systems are a real filter for supervisor roles.

Languages

International hotels hire on languages. They sit in the rail with a level indicator; keep the levels honest.

Photo

Hospitality is one of the sectors where a professional photo is common outside the US and UK. Use a plain background and the uniform-level smartness of the role.
