What finance managers look for

Qualifications, systems, and scale. Put your designation in the job title line ("Senior Accountant, CPA") so it is the first thing read, and repeat it with the issuer under Certifications & Licences.

Give every role a scale

Revenue, number of entities, close timeline, team size:

Own the month-end close for three entities and $90M revenue; reduced close from 8 days to 4 by automating accruals in NetSuite.

Audit roles: client sectors, revenue range, and whether you led fieldwork.

Systems and standards

The skills grid works well here: GAAP, IFRS, SOX, NetSuite, SAP, Oracle, Excel (modelling, Power Query), BlackLine. List the ERP from the job posting if you have used it — it is usually a filter.

Students and trainees

List exam progress precisely: "ACCA — 9 of 13 papers passed" or "CPA — FAR and AUD passed, REG scheduled". Specific progress reads as momentum.
