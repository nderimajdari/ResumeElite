How a federal resume differs

Federal HR specialists check your resume against the qualifications in the job announcement, line by line. If a requirement is not visibly on the page, it does not count, however obvious it is from your job title. So a federal resume is more explicit than a private-sector one.

For each job, include:

Job title, series and grade (if federal)    e.g. Program Analyst, GS-0343-11
Employer and city, state
Start and end dates                         month and year
Hours per week                              e.g. 40

Then describe the duties and results that match the announcement's specialized experience paragraph, using its wording where it is true for you.

Length

Federal resumes used to run four or five pages. Since September 2025, under OPM's Merit Hiring Plan, USAJOBS only accepts resumes of two pages or less, so the detail has to be precise rather than long. Always check the "How to apply" section of the announcement you are applying to, as requirements vary.

What to leave out

No photo, no date of birth, no Social Security number. Salary history is not needed for most announcements. Keep references off the resume unless asked.

State and local government

The same habits help: match the posting's language, give dates and hours, and make every required qualification easy to find.
