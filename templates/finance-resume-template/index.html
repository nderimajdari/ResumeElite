What finance recruiters look for

Accuracy, judgement and numbers. A finance resume that has vague bullets or a typo is read as a preview of your work. Keep the layout conservative and let precise, quantified results do the talking.

Bullets with financial impact

Name the model, the deal or the process, and the money or time it moved:

Built a three-statement model for a $450M acquisition; the board approved the deal at the valuation range recommended.

Automated month-end variance analysis in Python and Power BI; close shortened from 8 days to 5.

Covered 22 mid-cap industrials as part of a 3-person equity research team.

Certifications and licences

CFA (with level), CPA, ACCA, FRM and regulatory licences go in their own section near the top. For many roles they are a filter, so do not bury them.

Technical skills

Be specific: Excel (financial modelling, VBA), SQL, Python, Power BI, Bloomberg, Capital IQ, SAP. "Advanced Excel" on its own says little.

Banking applications

For investment banking analyst roles, use one page and put education first if you are a student. The Consulting template is a good alternative: the same dense, employer-first format the industry uses.
