The Consulting resume template

The high-density format used in management consulting and banking recruiting. One page, a serif name over a burgundy rule, employers listed first with your title in italics beneath, and dates right-aligned so the page reads as a tidy grid.

  • Best forManagement consulting, strategy, investment banking, MBA
  • StructureSingle column, employer-first entries, right-aligned dates
  • LengthOne page, strictly

What consulting recruiters screen for

Firms screen hundreds of resumes per role, against the same few signals: academic strength, quantified impact, leadership, and what consultants call a "spike", one thing you are clearly exceptional at. The format puts these where a screener looks: education at the top for students, employers in bold, and results in every bullet.

Bullets that pass a consulting screen

Each bullet is a small case: what you did, how, and the measurable result.

Built a pricing model across 2,400 SKUs that raised gross margin 3.2 points in two quarters.

Led a team of 4 analysts on a cost diagnostic; client adopted 11 of 14 recommendations, saving $18M a year.

Avoid "responsible for" and "assisted with". If you cannot put a number on it, show scale instead: team size, budget, number of markets or users.

Order of sections

For students and MBA candidates, Education comes first, with test scores or honours if they are strong. Experienced hires put Experience first. End with a short Additional line: languages, technical skills and two or three genuine interests. Interviewers do ask about them.

One page

Consulting and banking applications expect one page. No exceptions until you are very senior.
