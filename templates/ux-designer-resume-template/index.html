The resume's only job

For designers, the resume gets someone to open your portfolio. So it needs a portfolio link in the header and two case studies good enough to make the click obvious.

Writing a case-study card

Use the Projects section. Title, a one-line context (tool, type), and a single outcome:

Checkout redesign — Case study, Figma. Cut checkout drop-off 23% after five rounds of usability testing.

Two cards fit side by side. Add the case-study URL so it is clickable in the PDF.

Experience

For each role: product, platform, team you worked with, and one or two outcomes. Name research methods you actually ran — interviews, usability tests, card sorts — rather than a generic "user-centred design".

Skills

Figma, prototyping, design systems, user research, accessibility (WCAG), basic HTML/CSS. Keep it to what a hiring manager would test you on.

Colour

The default pink suits consumer products. Pick the accent closest to your own portfolio so the two feel like a set.
