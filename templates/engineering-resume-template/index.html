Engineering managers read for evidence

What did you design, under what constraints, and how do we know it worked? Every entry should answer at least two of those.

Designed drive assemblies for a 40,000-unit production run; FEA-led redesign cut bracket weight 18% with no change to fatigue life.

Technical skills rail

Group by type: CAD (SolidWorks, CATIA, AutoCAD, Revit), analysis (ANSYS, MATLAB), manufacturing (GD&T, DFM, lean), and codes and standards relevant to your discipline (ASME, Eurocodes, NEC).

Licences and chartership

PE, EIT, CEng or IEng belong in your title line and in certifications. If you are working towards chartership, say so with the expected date.

Engineering projects

The Projects section is titled Engineering Projects. Use it for capstone or competition work (Formula Student, robotics) early in your career, and for significant designs later on.
