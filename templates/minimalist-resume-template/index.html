The Minimal resume template

A type-only resume. A light, wide-set name, section labels in small capitals in the left margin, and plenty of white space. There are no lines, colours or icons, so it looks considered rather than plain.

  • Best forDesign-aware roles, consulting, tech, anyone who wants calm

Minimal is not the same as empty

A minimalist resume removes decoration, not information. The space it saves goes to white space, which makes every line easier to read. It works best when your content is already strong: clear titles, specific results, short bullets.

Make the content as clean as the layout

  • One idea per bullet. If a bullet needs "and" twice, split it.
  • Consistent dates. Same format everywhere, aligned to the right.
  • Short summary. Two lines at most.
  • Fewer, better skills. Six relevant skills look stronger than twenty.

Why the labels sit in the margin

Putting the section names in a left column creates a clean vertical line the eye can follow down the page, so the reader finds Experience or Education without hunting. The content column stays wide enough for full-length bullets.

Minimal vs Simple

Both are ATS-safe single-flow layouts. Simple has a centred name and ruled headings and feels conventional; Minimal has margin labels and light type and feels more designed. Try both in the editor; your content stays when you switch.
