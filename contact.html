Contact Us

We'd love to hear from you. Get in touch with our team.

Questions, bug reports, template requests and corrections all reach us through the form below or the email address it sends to. We read everything.

What we can help with

  • Something is broken. A template rendering oddly, a PDF exporting wrong, a section that will not save. Tell us your browser and which template, and we can usually reproduce it.
  • A template request. If your field has a formatting convention we have not covered, we want to know. Several of the fifty templates exist because someone asked.
  • A correction to a guide. If you hire for a living and something we have written is wrong or out of date, this is the most valuable message you can send us.
  • A privacy or data request. Covered by the privacy policy; we aim to respond within 30 days.

What we cannot help with

  • Reviewing or rewriting your resume. We do not offer a critique service, so please do not send your resume for feedback — we would rather say so than leave you waiting.
  • Recovering a lost draft. Your resume is stored only in your own browser and never reaches us, so there is no copy on our side to restore. Exporting a PDF when you finish a session is the safety net.
  • Job applications or referrals. We are not a recruiter or a job board.

Before you write about a bug

Two things solve most reports on the spot: try the same thing in a different browser, and check whether the exported PDF looks different from the on-screen preview. If it happens in both browsers, tell us which template and roughly how much content you had in it — page-break issues usually depend on length.
